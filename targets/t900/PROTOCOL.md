## 9. Imported from the removed combined target (2026-10-13)
Source: `targets/c900/sources/legacy-t900-c900-PROTOCOL.md`, written from a third-party write-up and the re-jieli skill, not from the HiWatch Pro app. Treat as [W] until S2 of this target.
- Jieli RCSP over service 0xAE00, write 0xAE01, notify 0xAE02 [W].
- Frame `FE DC BA`, flag (0xC0 typical: command, wants response), opcode, u16 big-endian body length, body starting with a sequence byte, trailer `EF` [W]; the same layout is [C] for a different Jieli product in reBeamBox (re-jieli skill).
- Self-made vector, status read: `FE DC BA C0 02 00 01 01 EF` [I] (own arithmetic, not from an artifact).
- E1/SAFER+ authentication may or may not be enforced by the T900 firmware [U]; HW read-only probe settles it.
- OTA services (0xAE10, 0xAE20 and similar) are never touched [W].
