## 2. Framing
| Frame | Header | Length | Payload | Checksum | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| app to watch | `DF` LenHi LenLo Chk Cmd `01` Sub PayLenHi PayLenLo | (Len << 8 \| LenLo) + 4 total | Payload | sum mod 256 of whole frame with byte 3 = 0 | [W] |
| watch ack | `FD 00 05` Chk Cmd Key AckLenHi AckLenLo `01` | fixed 9 | none | as above [I] | [W] |

Checksums: algorithm, parameters, check value of "123456789", where found.
