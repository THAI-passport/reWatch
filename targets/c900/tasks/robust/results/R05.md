## Summary
Health and fitness sync is coordinated via command 0x05 using a master-worker request/response flow.
Sync is triggered by the app via cmd 0x05 key 0x01 (triggered automatically on connection after key 0x06 real-time step ACK).
The watch delimits data transfer with key 7 (sync start) and key 8 (sync finish).
Every valid incoming DF frame requires an immediate FD ACK from the client before parsing.
Residual unknowns from C900-Q16 are resolved: raw distances are in meters (divided by 1000 for km), raw calories are in calories (divided by 1000 for kcal), sport modes map specific metrics to modeData1 (steps or reps) and modeData2 (distance in meters), and header byte `p[2]` is unused padding across all record parsers.
