## Summary
The vendor app configures BLE scanning with ScanMode.LOW_LATENCY and no GATT UUID filter, matching MAC in discovery callbacks.
On connection, service discovery requests MTU 32 (or 450 if DFU 5610) and posts a 1000 ms delay before enabling notifications on the Nordic TX characteristic.
Packet writes use 20-byte chunks spaced 20 ms apart, with 2 write retries (100 ms sleep between retries, 3 attempts total) and a 100 s ACK timeout; failure aborts by calling disconnect().
Reconnect is scheduled after 3000 ms on connection failure, or 10000 ms on unexpected disconnection (2000 ms after OTA success).
Classic BT bonding via 0x0E/1 runs asynchronously (35 s retry interval, max 3 attempts) and never pauses or gates the LE transport.
