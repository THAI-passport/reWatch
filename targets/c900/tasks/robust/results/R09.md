### PROTOCOL.md
Append new section at end of `targets/c900/PROTOCOL.md`:
```markdown
## 11. Hardware-confirmed behaviour (2026-10-13)

Static analysis was verified against physical C900 hardware across three live probe sessions and an Android HCI snoop capture on 2026-10-13:

1. **Advertisement and Identity [H]:**
   - Advertised Device Name: `'Watch Ultra'`.
   - Manufacturer Data: Company ID `0x314C` followed by 6-byte watch MAC and fixed trailer `12 34`.
2. **Transport & GATT Services [H]:**
   - Command transport is Nordic UART Service (`6e400001-b5a3-f393-e0a9-e50e24dcca9f`), writing to `...0002` and listening on `...0003`.
   - Characteristic `0x2A27` returns ASCII `"10000"`, establishing wire `ver = 0x10`.
   - Characteristic `0x2A28` returns a 27-byte feature vector starting with `0xAA`.
   - Characteristics `0xFF00`, `0xFF10`, `0xFF12` are present on firmware but untouched by companion app.
3. **Framing & Acknowledgments [H]:**
   - Inbound watch ACKs follow `FD 00 05 <chk> <cmd> <key> <x_hi> <x_lo> <status>` where `x = plen + 9` (the total length of the acknowledged frame).
   - Time synchronization uses year-2000 packed 4-byte encoding (`Frames.timePayload`), returning ACK `x=13`.
4. **Safe-Write Command Execution [H]:**
   - Find (`cmd 0x02 key 0x0B`): executes alert and vibration without per-connection bind handshake.
   - Message Push (`cmd 0x02 key 0x12`): 24-byte payload transmitted across two 20-byte ATT chunks displays text on screen.
   - Time Sync (`cmd 0x02 key 0x01`): updates system clock with status 1.
5. **Security & Link Layer [H]:**
   - Low Energy transport is completely unencrypted; no SMP pairing packets or LTKs are exchanged.
   - Inbound `cmd 0x0E key 1` announces the Classic Bluetooth MAC for audio pairing; open clients must drop or ignore it to prevent unauthorized pairing.
```
